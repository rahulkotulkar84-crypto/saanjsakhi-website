About SAANJSAKHI

SAANJSAKHI is more than just a fashion brand - it's a movement empowering women through entrepreneurship and quality apparel.


Established in April 2022, we specialize in manufacturing and trading premium women's clothing including blouses, gowns, sarees, kurtis, and lehengas.


Our mission is to create sustainable employment opportunities for women while delivering exceptional fashion that celebrates Indian culture and modern design.
